Not deployed. Adds per-unit responsible employee (active same-dealership users only, with assignment history), a dealership inventory-review interval (default 30, 7-90 days) computed on dashboard/priorities load with deterministic escalation after a second ignored interval, a "Confirm Current Status" review action, and structured dispositions (retail sold uses `sold`; wholesale, traded, auction, junked, returned, and duplicate use `archived`) kept as append-only history with corrections superseding, never deleting. Retail sold and changes to sold units need `inventory_pricing`; amounts are hidden without it and never enter the activity timeline or public routes. Sold and archived are reachable only through a disposition: direct stage changes, the dashboard alert, unit creation, and imports refuse them (409 or skipped row), and closing a deal records a retail-sold disposition (no amount) for its linked active unit in the same transaction. The unit's sale/archive fields and pricing comparables always mirror the latest disposition; reopening a sold or archived unit clears its sale/archive fields and comparable, and marks any disposition reversed. Units with assignment or disposition history cannot be permanently deleted. No history is fabricated for legacy sold/archived units. Activity times now read SQLite UTC timestamps correctly. No scheduler, AI, or messaging. Open product decision: whether a retail-sold disposition should close a linked pending deal (currently it does not).
